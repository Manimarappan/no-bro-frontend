Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

  • We collect your userId, firstName, lastName, email, phoneNumber, userType, and profilePic (JPEG image as base64).
  • Data is provided when you create or update your profile.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

  • To manage your user profile and display information like your profile picture and email.
  • To provide property-related features (e.g., viewing liked properties).

3. Data Storage

  • Your data is stored securely in our database (e.g., profilePic as base64 in MEDIUMTEXT).
  • We use localStorage to store your userId for session management.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not share your data with third parties except for hosting services (e.g., backend server).

5. Data Security

  • We use HTTPS for API calls (e.g., profile updates) and hash passwords for security.
  • We enforce a 1 MB limit for profile picture uploads to prevent issues.

6. Your Rights

  • You can update your profile data via the Profile page.
  • Contact us to request account deletion.
